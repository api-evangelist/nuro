https://medium.com/media/a206577006e75c40e4de827d91c4352c/href In this blog post, we present LAMBDA, Nuro’s Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM). LAMBDA performs question answering as part of The Nuro Driver’s™ onboard autonomy stack, running in real-time on the hardware inside our autonomous vehicle platforms. LAMBDA has shown to be extremely capable of reasoning about a wide variety of challenging situations that an L4 driverless system is required to handle.
